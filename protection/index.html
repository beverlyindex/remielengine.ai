What Remiel does

Your phone becomes the sensor.

Remiel listens to the Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and cell signals around you and turns them into a picture you can read at a glance.

Every device around you

Every Bluetooth and Wi-Fi device in range, nearest first, with its distance, its maker and the kind of device it is.

Know what follows you

A device that moves with you is marked in red. Remiel alerts you the moment a device travels with you, or appears again in a different place.

Trackers, cameras and drones

Tracker tags, plate cameras and drones are named as they are heard. Remiel also tells you when your phone is moved onto an older 2G cell connection.

Trips that are kept

Start, mark and end a journey. Every trip is saved with its route and every device along it, so you can compare one trip with the next and print any of them.

Defend against attackers

A register of AI tools, spyware and trackers, each rated from 1 to 5 for you, with step-by-step defenses for the phone you carry and a Device Health Check.

Evidence you can hand over

A dated record of every finding, every defense completed and every trip, with your own notes timed as you write them, ready to export.

How it works

Three steps. No extra hardware.

Open Remiel

Install it from Google Play and open it. Remiel starts listening right away and shows every device around you.

Go about your day

Walk, ride or drive. Start a trip and Remiel records each device along the way, even with your screen off.

See what changed

Review the trip. Remiel shows what came closer, what stayed with you, and what it has met before somewhere else.

Privacy

Your record stays with you.

Remiel is built so that what it learns about the world around you belongs to you, and no one else.

Read the full privacy notice

  • The record of the devices around you stays on your phone.
  • Our server holds no name, email address or password for your account.
  • What syncs between your own devices is encrypted on your device, with a key our server does not hold.
  • No advertising, no analytics, and no tracking cookies.
  • We do not sell your information.
